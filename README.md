📘 Daily Planner — Aesthetic Study & Routine Tracker

A soft sky-blue, aesthetic daily study planner built as a lightweight, single-page web app. Plan your routine every day with automatic browser-side saving — no login, no accounts, and zero setup required.

✨ Features

🎯 Purposeful Start & Finish: Set Today’s Goal to begin your day with focus and record Today’s Wins to reflect on your achievements.

⏰ Comprehensive Hourly Schedule: Plan and time-block your study sessions hourly from 4:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

✅ Focused Task Management:

Top 5 Priorities to highlight non-negotiable tasks

Flexible To-Do List

Subjects to Study breakdown

Dedicated Don’t Forget reminder box

💗 Weekly Study Tracker: Keep tabs across key study habits: Focus, Notes, Practice, Revision, Reading, and Mocks.

🎀 Bow Tracker (Pomodoro Timer): Built-in interval timer with 25 min focus, 5 min short break, and an extended long break after completing 4 bows.

💧 Wellness & Mindfulness Trackers:

Water intake counter

⭐ Day rating

General quick notes

🐻 "I’m proud of" self-appreciation box

📅 Historical Date Navigation: Easily jump to past dates to review, edit, or update previous entries.

💾 Automatic Local Persistence: Real-time autosave to browser localStorage with a manual Save button and a Clear Day reset option.

🌙 Responsive & Aesthetic Design: Soft sky-blue visual theme, automatic dark mode support, and a mobile-friendly layout.

🛠️ Built With

HTML5 — Semantic, clean structure

CSS3 — Custom styling, soft sky-blue palette & responsive layout

Vanilla JavaScript — Timer logic, scheduling, date navigation, and localStorage integration
